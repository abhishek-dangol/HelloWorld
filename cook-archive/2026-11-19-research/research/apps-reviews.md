# Third-party iOS camera apps: what people praise and complain about

Research limits (read first): web search only, about 8 minutes. Reddit, Blackmagic forum and aggregator pages (marlvel, kimola) mostly returned 403/410, so I could NOT read Reddit threads or review counts directly. Most evidence is a handful of App Store reviews, two tech reviews, MacRumors reader comments, and third-party AI review aggregators. Counts below are rough, from what I actually saw, NOT statistics. Apps with no usable data: Snow, B612, BeautyPlus, Lapse, Hipstamatic, Leica LUX, Project Indigo, Final Cut Camera, NOMO, Focos, ProCamera (only a one-line "UI feels dated" note).

## Complaints (ranked by how often seen)
1. Price / subscription / paywall (about 8 mentions, clearly #1)
   - Halide: backlash when it moved from one-time purchase to subscription (kimola summary, MacRumors comments). Current options seen on App Store page: $19.99, $9.99 (year), $69.99 one-time; 7-day trial.
   - Kino: commenters fear it will copy Halide and go subscription; others say Blackmagic is free so why pay $19.99.
   - Filmic Pro: subscription backlash named as main concern (marlvel, unverified AI summary).
   - Camera+ 2: user left after paid-update model (Apple Community thread).
   - Dazz Cam: aggregator says about 30% of reviewers mention that nice cameras are VIP-only (unverified method). One reviewer paid lifetime and says app then stopped working.
   - VSCO: bugs, subscription cost, few free features (kimola summary).
   - General photo apps: aggressive paywalls, watermarks, ads in free tiers (unstar.app blog, "about 35% of 1-star reviews", methodology unverified).
2. Bugs / not working as promised (about 5 mentions)
   - Halide (2020 review, old): crashes in depth photos, photos not saved, crashes switching lenses. Developer admitted it.
   - Kino: "frequent crashes and freezes" per marlvel (49 reviews, AI summary, unverified).
   - Blackmagic Camera: forum has a 2025 "Issues/Bugs" thread (could not open it).
   - Dazz Cam: app stopped working after purchase.
   - Kino commenter: Blackmagic is "more stable" (opinion).
3. Missing features (about 4)
   - Kino: only four fixed zoom steps (0.5/1/2/3x), no smooth manual zoom; feels thin versus Blackmagic (RedShark).
   - Kino: looks are baked in, switching to ProRes when on (RedShark).
   - Halide: RAW export hard (2020 review).
4. Confusing UI (about 3)
   - Halide (3-star 2020 review): cluttered icons, hidden control screens, misleading name "Instant RAW" that is really processed.
   - ProCamera: UI feels older than Halide/LUX (one blog).
   - Photo-app aggregator: confusing UI for casual users.
5. Ads (about 1-2): only in generic free-tier photo-app analysis. Privacy: nothing found. Overheating, lost recordings, blur, slow shutter: nothing specific found in this pass (not proof they are absent).

## Praise (what 5-star reviews and reviewers love)
1. Looks great out of the box / film-style grading (Kino "Instant Grade" called the star; Dazz Cam vintage look; about 4 mentions).
2. One-time price, no subscription (Kino "instant purchase", $19.99; Blackmagic free; about 4 mentions).
3. Easy for beginners but with pro controls (Kino; Halide "clean and calm" per a beginner; about 3).
4. Pro controls that work: lock exposure and white balance to stop colour shifts in pans (Kino); RAW/JPEG side by side (Halide).
5. Clean exports and active updates: files drop into Final Cut/LumaFusion without trouble; developers ship fixes fast (Kino, Halide).
6. Free yet full-featured (Blackmagic Camera).

## Top 5 complaints a new camera app could win on
1. Honest pricing: one-time buy or a truly free core, no weekly plans, no "VIP-only" looks, a promise not to move existing buyers to subscription.
2. Never lose a recording or photo; stability (crash, freeze, unsaved file).
3. Smooth manual zoom and the basic controls rivals lack, not just fixed steps.
4. Plain labels and a UI that matches the iPhone Camera: no hidden control screens or misleading feature names.
5. Looks that are not baked in (keep a clean original) and no forced format switch.

## Top 5 loved things a new app must not be missing
1. Good-looking one-tap looks/grades that need no editing.
2. A fair, no-subscription price (or free).
3. Simple for beginners with pro controls available (exposure/white-balance lock).
4. Reliable, clean files that import into other editors.
5. Frequent visible updates from the developer.
