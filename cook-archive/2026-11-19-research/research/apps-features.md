## Headline features per group
- Halide: RAW/ProRAW, Process Zero (no Apple processing), manual focus + peaking, histogram, depth, macro, Looks (film-style alternative processes), HDR, built-in editor. Chosen for control and true-to-sensor look.
- Kino: manual exposure/focus, focus peaking, waveform scopes, LUT import, filmmaker color presets applied while recording, free lessons. Chosen for cinematic video simple.
- Blackmagic Camera: Log/ProRes, manual everything, scopes, false color, Blackmagic Cloud upload, free. Chosen as free pro video.
- ProCamera: 4K/120fps, ProRes RAW, RAW photos, manual controls, long exposure.
- Leica LUX: Leica lens simulations (Summilux 28 f/1.4 free), Leica Looks, manual controls, pro-camera dial UI.
- Project Indigo: multi-frame HDR, super-res zoom, RAW/JPEG, manual controls, Lightroom hand-off, no account.
- Final Cut Camera: manual video, LOG, ProRes, multicam with iPad, Final Cut handoff.
- Dazz / NOMO / OldRoll / 1998 Cam / radcam: instant retro film or CCD look at capture, date stamp, grain/leaks, double exposure, video styles, no editing needed.
- Lapse: disposable-camera feel, photos "develop" later, friends feed.
- Snow / B612 / BeautyPlus / SODA / Meitu: live face beautify, AR stickers, AI filters, AI profile photos.
- Hipstamatic: virtual lens + film combos, flash, analog feel.
- Focos: depth map, refocus/bokeh after the shot.
- VSCO: preset library, editing tools, community.

## (a) Features nearly all have
Live filter/look preview before shooting; front/back toggle; photo + video; grid/level; some manual or at least exposure control (pro apps); in-app editor or filters; free download with paywall on extras; export/share.

## (b) Features only the best sellers have (by ratings count / revenue signals)
- Retro/film-look as the core hook with real-time preview (Dazz, NOMO, Lapse: 48k-118k ratings).
- Lots of looks sold as a Pro pack (Dazz Pro, Halide Looks, Leica Looks).
- Date stamp, light leaks, dust, double exposure (Dazz).
- Social/sharing loop (Lapse).
- AI beautify / AI profile (BeautyPlus 349k, B612 110k, Snow).
- Pro tier: RAW/Log/ProRes, scopes, LUT import (Halide, Kino, Blackmagic).
- Brand/lens simulation (Leica).
- Free-to-download with trial subscription.

## (c) Onboarding (mostly not verified)
- Dazz Cam: first screens are camera and photo-library permission requests (~0:00 and ~0:10); onboarding seemed to run ~8 screens in the first 28 s; "Unlock Pro" paywall screens appear from ~0:31 and again while browsing effects (~1:19), so the paywall is repeated and tied to locked effects, not one hard gate. Trial terms not verified.
- Halide: short feature tour (about 2 steps), then soft paywall (dismissible) at ~1:00 with a 1-week free trial on the annual plan.
- LoFi Cam: funnels to a paywall by ~0:37; ism: soft paywall with 3-day trial after a feature showcase.
- Category analysis (1,200 paywalls): weekly prices cluster $4.99-$9.99, annual $35.99-$99.99, trials 3 or 7 days mostly on weekly plans, annual often no trial, lifetime rare (anchor), paywalls usually after a success moment (export, remove watermark) rather than in onboarding.
- Others (Kino, Blackmagic, Leica, Indigo, Lapse, NOMO, VSCO, B612): not verified.
