# CinematiCam: proposals from the camera-app research (2026-11-18, 15-minute pass)

Evidence is thin on reviews (Reddit and review-summary sites were blocked). Ratings are live App Store numbers; revenue ranks not verified. Details: apps-features.md, apps-reviews.md.

## What the research says
- Biggest sellers by rating count are "one tap and it already looks good" apps: BeautyPlus 349k, VSCO 279k, Dazz Cam 118k, Lapse 118k, B612 110k. Pro apps are far smaller (Halide 14k, Kino 2k).
- Top complaint by far: price. Subscriptions, looks locked behind "VIP", paywalls popping up within 30 s of first launch (Dazz), one-time buyers moved to subscriptions (Halide).
- Second: things that break (crashes, photos not saved).
- Third: confusing screens (unlabelled icons, hidden controls).
- Most loved: one-tap looks, fair one-time price, simple with depth behind it, reliable saving.

## Proposals
1. First-launch welcome, 3 screens, each a real before/after from our own camera: Looks, Background blur + skin + Mist, Video tools (teleprompter, captions, editor). Then the camera/mic/photos asks, each with one line saying why. No price shown.
2. Tidy the camera screen: small word under each top icon the first few times; Done button in Settings; "Speed" renamed "Frames per second"; camera-denied alert gets an "Open Settings" button; shorter Mirror selfies wording.
3. Fix photo reliability first (already measured): shoot at the tap, blink at the real moment. Reliability is complaint no. 2 for rivals.
4. Pricing stance (decision, not code): one fair one-time price, every look included, never a subscription; say so on the App Store page. This is the gap rivals leave open.
5. Later ideas: date stamp + film grain look (Dazz/NOMO hook), smooth zoom while recording (Kino lacks it), save the untouched original next to the filtered photo.

## Found in our app
- No onboarding, no paywall. Top-bar icons unlabelled. Settings has no Done. Speech denial is silent. Vintage/Vivid filter files look unused (unverified).
